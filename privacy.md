# Formula Trainer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

This policy applies to the Formula Trainer app ("the app"), a reflex training game provided by Senan Batu Çobanoğlu. It explains what information is handled when you use it.

## Short version
- The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
- Your scores, records, medals, streak and settings are stored **only on your device**. We do not run a server and never receive them.
- The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.
- The optional "No Ads" purchase is handled entirely by **Apple**. We never see your payment details.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves the following on your device so it works between sessions:
- best scores and the last 20 results for each drill,
- medals, daily streak and daily drill status,
- sound, haptics and language settings,
- whether you own the "No Ads" purchase, and a count of rounds played (used to space out ads).

This information stays on your device and may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backup. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
If you have not bought "No Ads", the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
- device information such as device model, operating system version and app version,
- identifiers such as the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and IP address (which can indicate an approximate location),
- ad interaction data such as which ads were shown or tapped,
- diagnostic data such as crash and performance information from the ads software.

The app does **not** request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies:
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before ads use your information where the law requires it.

## Purchases
The "No Ads" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app only receives confirmation that you own the purchase.

## Sharing results
If you tap "Share" on a result, the app creates an image of your result on your device and opens the system share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where to share it.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## Children
The app is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices
- Buying "No Ads" stops ads from being shown in the app.
- On iPhone you can reset or limit ad-related identifiers in Settings → Privacy & Security.
- Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.
