# Formula Trainer Support

Formula Trainer is a reflex training game with seven short drills: start lights, speed, focus, two hands, side vision, sequence memory and ball tracking.

## Contact
Questions, problems or refund help: **batuna07@gmail.com**

## Restoring "No Ads"
"No Ads" is a one-time purchase. On a new or reset device, open Formula Trainer, tap **Remove ads** at the bottom of the home screen and then **Restore purchases**, while signed in to the same App Store account.

## Your progress
Scores, medals and your streak are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
